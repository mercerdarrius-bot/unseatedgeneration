Miracles Greater Than Jesus? What John 14:12 Really Means

John 14:12 · NASB15 minNovember 10, 2026
Watch on YouTube (opens in a new tab)
About This Episode

"Greater works than these" is one of the most quoted lines in church and one of the most misunderstood. Did Jesus really promise you would perform miracles greater than His?

This episode takes John 14:12 back to the room it was spoken in: a table, eleven grieving men, and a Savior hours from the cross.

Read in context, the greater works are not bigger signs and wonders. They are the gospel reaching further than Jesus' earthly ministry ever did, through the Holy Spirit after Pentecost. The works are greater because of what Jesus did, not because of who we are becoming.

If you have ever been told something is wrong with your faith because the miracle didn't come, this one is for you.
