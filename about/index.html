About
Unseated Generation.

Unseated Generation was not built around a personality. It was built around a burden. We exist to strengthen believers answering God's call, restore those whose devotion has grown weary, and reach people who do not yet know Christ. That assignment has not changed since 2022. What deepened is our understanding of what stands in its way.

SEAT
The SeatThe Name

Something Has Taken the Seat.

At the center of the Christian life is a question of lordship. What actually governs us? Jesus does not ask to be included among the important things. He is Lord, and whatever occupies the place that belongs to Him has to be unseated.

Little of what competes for that place looks like rebellion. It arrives as tradition, as gifting, as approval, as comfort, as ministry itself. Some of it we inherited before we knew enough to examine it. Some of it we kept because it affirmed what we wanted to believe about ourselves.

What has taken the seat in the church? In our theology? In our culture? And what has taken it in me?

That last question is the one that costs something. Before we ask where a pastor confused gifting with maturity, we ask where we have. Before we critique a culture built on visibility, we ask why being unseen makes us feel insignificant. We never use Scripture to confront what is around us without letting Scripture confront what is in us.

CALL
The CallThe Assignment

Called. Refined. Sent.

Three words carry how the work moves, and they move in order. Confrontation is part of the process. It was never the destination.

01
Called
Before we are called to a platform, an assignment, or a purpose, we are called to Christ. God calls us out of the identities we built for ourselves and into relationship with Him. Everything else follows from that.
02
Refined
God does not only intend to correct what we believe. He intends to transform who we are. Truth is not collected so we can win arguments. It is submitted to so we can become disciples.
03
Sent
Formation turns outward or it was never formation. We are sent to proclaim Christ and make His love tangible: evangelism, discipleship, serving our city, strengthening families, investing in students, meeting real needs.

The StoryThe Founder

Before Unseated Was a Message, It Was Personal.

Darrius Mercer
Founder · Unseated Generation

Unseated Generation began in prayer in 2022. Darrius Mercer received a name and an assignment before there was anything built to carry either one, and the first version of this ministry was built around it.

Then the work went quiet. He and Carrington walked through loss and a season of hardship that asked everything they had, and the platform was set down for what mattered more. What looked like an interruption is where this ministry was formed.

In those years Darrius began testing beliefs he had been handed and never questioned. Some held. Some needed context he had never been given. Others had to be surrendered. What mattered most was not finding things around him that needed correction. It was finding things in him that needed to be unseated. Unseated happened to him before it became anything he said to anyone else.

Called to confront systems. Formed to love. Assigned to build.
