Truth on Trial · Episode 2

Can You Be Christian and Pro-Choice?

Psalm 139:13-16 · ESV91 minNovember 24, 2026
Watch on YouTube (opens in a new tab)
About This Episode

Can you be Christian and support abortion? Spenser Jones, Carrington Mercer, Darrius Mercer and Chris White take the question to Scripture: what the Bible says about life in the womb (Luke 1, Jeremiah 1:5, Psalm 139) and whether it calls abortion a sin (Proverbs 6, Exodus 20:13).

Then the hardest cases: rape, and a mother's life at stake. The panel does not agree on everything, and the conversation stays honest about it.

From there it turns to the church itself: the gossip, hypocrisy and abuse that keep people from trusting the church enough to hear what the Bible says, and how that trust is won back.

It closes with the gospel, healing for anyone carrying an abortion or the trauma of assault, and a prayer for anyone facing the decision.
