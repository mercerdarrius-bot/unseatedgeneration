Privacy

This site uses Merx Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Merx Analytics does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses, and does not follow you across other websites. It records the pages visited, links and buttons clicked, whether a form was submitted (never what was written in it), the website that referred you, your general device, browser, and screen size, and your approximate location (city, state, or country). This information is used only in aggregate to improve the site.

What you write in the interest form on the Get Involved page is used only by Unseated Generation to follow up with you about where you fit.
