SENT
The Mission

Faith That Shows Up.
Christ's Love, Tangible.

Unseated Generation exists to confront the systems, mindsets, and patterns that prevent spiritual maturity and limit kingdom impact, and to build the kind of infrastructure that meets people where they are. Teaching forms us. Being sent proves it.

The same Word that confronts us in private is the Word that sends us into our neighborhoods to serve, to build, and to love without condition. We welcome and serve everyone. That is what Sent means.

The Work

Where We're Headed.

These are the lanes we are building, the places where formation becomes action. We are at the very beginning, and that is exactly the point. Come build them with us.

01
Spiritual Formation & Discipleship
Teaching and discipleship that forms people in the truth of Scripture, not the performance of culture.
02
Youth & Collegiate Outreach
Investing in the next generation early, before the world tells them who to be.
03
Financial Literacy
Equipping families and individuals to steward money with wisdom and freedom.
04
Family Support
Strengthening households as the first place faith is lived and passed down.
05
Housing & Stability
Working toward stability for people walking through seasons of transition.
06
Mental & Emotional Health
Caring for the whole person, because formation includes the mind and the heart.
07
Feeding & Practical Care
Meeting tangible needs with dignity, because love is also bread on the table.
08
Economic Opportunity
Opening doors to work, skills, and dignity that last.
